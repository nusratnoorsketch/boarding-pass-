Option 1 · live

Connect the Google Form's response sheet

On your own website (GitHub Pages): paste the sheet's Publish to web CSV link. It ends in output=csv. The board checks it about every 15 seconds.

Opened inside Claude instead: you can paste the sheet's normal link. The board then reads it through your Google Drive connection, about every 30 seconds.

This setup screen shows the sheet link only, never names. Even so, set it up before the projector goes on.

Option 2 · backup

Paste the facts yourself

Microsoft Forms: open the responses in Excel, press Ctrl + A then Ctrl + C, and paste here. The board picks out the first-name and fact columns by itself. Or type one fact per line as Name | Fact. Use this if the Google link isn't available on the day.
